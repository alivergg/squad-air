2. Voluntary Use of the Public Coordinator (rv.squadair.app)

The public coordinator at rv.squadair.app:3478 is provided free of charge as a best-effort community signaling endpoint for UDP hole punching, X25519 friend presence pings, SPAKE2 short-code handshakes, and low-bandwidth voice/chat CGNAT relay fallback.

You are never required to use rv.squadair.app. You may switch to your own squad-rendezvous instance under Settings at any time or operate purely over local area networks using LAN Mode.

3. Fair & Acceptable Use of Public Infrastructure

When connecting to rv.squadair.app, you agree not to abuse shared UDP resources or target other users. Strictly prohibited activities include:

  • Sending spoofed source IP packets, volumetric UDP floods, or denial-of-service traffic toward the coordinator or peer endpoints.
  • Automated enumeration or brute-force guessing of 6-digit SPAKE2 voice codes.
  • Attempting to bypass Tier 3 relay packet size (320 bytes) or bandwidth caps to tunnel video streams or bulk files through the public relay.
  • Using SquadAir infrastructure for unlawful harassment, unauthorized network scanning, or distribution of malware.

To preserve stability for active squads, squad-rendezvous automatically enforces per-IP signaling token buckets (30 packets/sec), short-code lockout windows (5 failed attempts triggers a 60-second block), and per-peer relay limits (150 packets/sec).
